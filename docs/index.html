Where your money really is

fund look-through

IBKR reports this account as United States / Broad because all the funds are US-domiciled. That is a custody fact, not an exposure fact. Regional splits come from config.json.

Gain on cost is not total return. Reinvested dividends buy more shares, which raises the cost basis without showing up as a gain, so for dividend-paying funds that column sits below the true total return by roughly the yield since purchase. Portfolio-level returns on the Performance page do include dividends; they come from the NAV series.

Drift from target

Targets come from config.json. Rebalancing here is buy-only: it never suggests selling, it points your next contribution at whatever has fallen furthest behind. That keeps you off a taxable event and, while you are still adding money monthly, is usually enough to hold the allocation in line on its own.

Portfolio / Performance

Money-weighted returns

what your money earned, not what the funds did

IBKR reports this account as TWR. These figures are money-weighted: external transfers are reconstructed from the NAV and return series, then an internal rate of return is solved over the result. MWR answers “how did the money I actually had invested do”, which is the number that matters when you are still contributing heavily.

Currency

Your funds are priced in one currency and you spend another. The reported return is the asset return multiplied by the exchange-rate move, and the two legs are separated below. These figures are time-weighted, because splitting a return into asset and currency legs only works on a series that ignores deposits.

What this does and does not measure. The split is exact for the pair shown, but that pair is the funds’ reporting currency.
